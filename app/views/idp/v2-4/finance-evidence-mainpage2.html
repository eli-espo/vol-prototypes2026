Financial evidence

To be granted a licence you need to prove you have enough money to keep your vehicles and run your business.

If you submit bank statements they must:

  • be in the name of the person/organisation applying for this licence
  • cover the 28 day period immediately before the date you submit this application
  • show itemised transactions as well as the opening and closing balance

You must be able to explain any large or unusual deposits to the account during the 28 day period

other ways to provide financial evidence (opens in new tab).

How much will you need?

£ 152,000

This figure is based on:

  • this application for 2 vehicles
  • maintenance of 31 vehicles on your other licence(s)
  • your other application(s) for 12 vehicles

How did we calculate this?

This amount is calculated using the following values:

! Warning You must provide original PDFs downloaded from your bank or provider. Your application may be rejected if you upload non-original files.

If you are scanning printed documents, you must:

  • Scan each document separately and upload it as an individual file
  • Not combine multiple documents into a single scan
  • Use scanned copies instead of photographs wherever possible
  • Make sure all scanned documents are clear and legible
